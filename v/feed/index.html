RECONCILIATION NOTES

Token precision: Both Tempo (chainId=4217, OUSD) and Base (chainId=8453, USDC) report reward amounts in 6-decimal atomic integers (1000000 = $1.00).

Platform-wide vs. chain-scoped fields: On /api/market/stats, only paidWorkers and activeWallets vary by chainId. registeredWorkers, activeWorkers7d, activeAgents7d, openTasks, /api/stats/breakdowns, /api/stats/platform-time-series, /api/stats/activity-heatmap, and /api/stats/activity-feed report platform-wide figures and are never summed across chains.

Count reconciliation: /api/stats/breakdowns reports 2,359 total platform tasks, while paginating active /api/tasks for Tempo (1,519) and Base (690) lists 2,209 indexed tasks. Similarly, openTasks (76) reflects currently active open tasks on /api/market/stats, whereas raw status=open rows in /api/tasks include tasks awaiting expiry transition.

Weekly distinct posters: Computed deterministically from paginated /api/tasks?chainId=4217 and 8453 by grouping lowercase requester addresses into ISO-8601 UTC weeks.

Privacy & read-only guarantee: No wallet connection, no browser calls to api.taskmarket.dev, no cookies, no analytics or tracking scripts, and no guessed names attached to wallet addresses.
